# AMAZON PRIME – CART ABANDONMENT & CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION

## 1. Project Overview

This project is about analysing Amazon Prime shopping sessions to understand why customers leave without completing their purchases.

I used Excel and SQL to clean the data, calculate important business metrics, analyse customer segments and create a dashboard.

**Analysis Period:** 1 October 2025 – 31 October 2025

## 2. Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- SQL (SQLite)
- PivotTables and PivotCharts

## 3. Project Objectives

- Understand customer shopping behaviour.
- Identify where customers leave the shopping process.
- Compare Prime and Non-Prime customers.
- Analyse abandonment by device and traffic source.
- Identify customer segments based on cart value.
- Estimate the revenue lost through abandoned carts.

## 4. Project Work

**Step 1 – Data Cleaning**

Checked missing values, duplicates, incorrect formats and unusual values. Created additional columns to support the analysis.

**Step 2 – Data Analysis**

Used Excel PivotTables and SQL queries to calculate conversion rate, cart abandonment rate, average abandoned cart value and revenue at risk.

**Step 3 – Customer Segmentation**

Grouped customers into Browsers, Purchasers, Low-Value Abandoners, Mid-Value Abandoners and High-Value Abandoners.

**Step 4 – Dashboard**

Created KPI cards and charts to present the main findings in a simple way.

## 5. Main Findings

- Overall conversion rate: 33.60%
- Cart abandonment rate: 50.37%
- Average abandoned cart value: $142.51
- Revenue at risk: $46,886.91
